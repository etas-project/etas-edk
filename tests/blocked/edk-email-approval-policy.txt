edk-email approval policy templates are blocked until package-defined action
scope metadata can represent `EdkEmail.send` and policy metadata can
publish package policy templates against those scoped actions.

Required architecture behavior:

- sending mail is high impact;
- suggested package policy templates require `Approval.request` before
  `EdkEmail.send`;
- tests prove the policy denies or pauses send without approval.

Rejected workaround:

Do not publish an unscoped policy that treats all email accounts as one
authority, and do not implement approval inside `send` as ordinary control flow.
Approval requirements belong in policy/action metadata, not a package-private
delivery handler.
