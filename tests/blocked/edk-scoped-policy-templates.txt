Reusable EDK allow/require policy templates are blocked until package metadata
can export package-defined action families and runtime payload predicates.

Safe deny-only templates are source-present under each integration package's
`policies/` module. They do not grant authority and do not register handlers.

Source-local HTTP policy fixtures cover the static action family. Exact
method/host policy matching is blocked on runtime payload inspection:

- `std-requirements/http/positive/scoped_policy_allow_get` accepts
  `EdkHttp.request`;
- `std-requirements/http/negative/scoped_policy_deny_post` rejects
  `EdkHttp.request`.
- `std-requirements/http/positive/root_wrapper_exact_policy_scope/probe.es`
  is the package-local target probe for exact
  `EdkHttp.request` coverage over root
  `edk.http.get(...)` and direct `edk.http.api.get(...)`.

Root `edk.http.get/post/...` wrapper calls now route through the single public
`request(req)` execution path. Direct `edk.http.api.get/post/...` calls use the
same path. EDK now records the static action family `EdkHttp.request`;
method/host proof is a runtime payload policy/replay concern. Keep the target
fixtures without publishing broad host allow templates or reintroducing direct
per-method `perform` calls in the wrappers.

Still-blocked reusable templates include:

- exported `EdkHttp.request` allowlists for package/caller
  configured methods and hosts;
- `EdkWorkspace.read<R>`, `write<R>`, and `list<R>` path scopes;
- `EdkEmail.send` requiring `Approval.request` before delivery;
- `EdkDb.query` read-only datasource allowlists;
- `EdkVector.search` and `EdkEmbedding.embed` scoped allowlists;
- `EdkBrowser.navigate`, `click`, and `read` payload-aware browser policies;
- `EdkGit.read` and `EdkGit.write` repository scopes;
- `EdkGitHub.issue_create<R>`, `pr_comment<R>`, and `read<R>` scopes;
- `EdkPdf.read`, `EdkDocs.convert`, and `EdkEval.write`
  scopes.

Do not replace these with broad unscoped allow policies. Broad allow templates
would make the EDK policy surface look complete while hiding the host, path,
account, datasource, store, repository, format, or suite boundary required by
the architecture.
