This is not a fallback browser handler. Browser protocol execution remains a
package-owned action boundary awaiting WebDriver/CDP/WebSocket substrate and
package-defined domain scope metadata.

`edk.browser.tools.page` and `edk.browser.mocks.browser` have been added as the
architected tool/mock layer. The pure smoke/fixture also cover conservative URL
path rejection for raw spaces, tabs, fragments, backslashes, and embedded
absolute URLs without invoking browser protocol execution. They parse in
source-level verification and package-mode smoke.
