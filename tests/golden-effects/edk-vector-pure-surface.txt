The `edk_vector_pure_surface` fixture covers deterministic vector helper
modules only:

- integer embedding construction, malformed dimension rejection, model dimension
  checks, and conservative model token rejection;
- dot product, L1 distance, and squared L2 distance;
- chunking policy validation, line chunk construction, line-only policy chunk
  aggregation, overlong-line isolation, and rejection of overlap policies for
  the line-only helper;
- metadata filter matching, wildcard filters, value-only filter rejection, and
  unsafe filter key/value rejection;
- store/model/record/query shape validation, including unsafe store identity,
  unsafe record IDs, invalid metadata key/value pairs, non-positive `top_k`, and
  oversized `top_k` rejection;
- vector query construction and record/query dimension compatibility checks;
- deterministic mock hit/result/write receipt constructors.

Expected EDK authority actions: none.
Expected std authority actions: none.
Expected checked errors:

- `Error<IndexError>`, from bounded array access in similarity and fixture
  assertions.

This fixture intentionally does not call `edk.vector.embed.embed`,
`edk.vector.store.search`, `edk.vector.store.upsert`, `edk.vector.retrieve`, or
`edk.vector.tools.retrieve.*`, because those expose runtime-mediated
`EdkEmbedding.embed` and `EdkVector.*` actions and require provider/store
handlers.
